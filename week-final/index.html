Made for Your Taste

Spotify builds your personal soundtrack from the moment you sign up. Based on your listening habits, it creates Recommended Stations, curated mixes, and artist radio tailored specifically to you. The more you listen, the smarter it gets — surfacing new artists you'll love while keeping your favorite sounds front and center.

More Than Just Music

Spotify is more than a music app. With Premium, you get unlimited access to millions of audiobooks and thousands of podcasts — all in one place. Whether you're into true crime, self-help, fiction, or daily news, there's always something worth listening to. Start a book on your commute and finish it on your couch, without missing a beat.
